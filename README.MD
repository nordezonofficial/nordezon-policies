# 🛡️ Nordezon Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 19th November 2025
**Entity:** Nordezon Tech (“Nordezon”, “we”, “our”, or “us”)

Nordezon Tech operates the Nordezon mobile application (“App”), a content-commerce platform where content-driven shopping meets culture, storytelling, and community engagement.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use the Nordezon mobile app, website, or any related services (collectively, the “Platform”).

> By using the Platform, you agree to this Privacy Policy and consent to the handling of your data as described below.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services and experiences for all users.

### a. Account and Identity Information

**Brand Accounts**

* Full name
* Email
* Contact number
* City & address
* Device token
* Government-issued ID (if verification is needed)
* Brand name & category
* Social media links
* Logo & banners
* Business website

**User Accounts**

* Full name
* Email
* Contact number
* City & address
* Category (optional)
* Device token
* Government-issued ID (if required)

> During signup, your phone number or email may be verified via a one-time password (OTP) to ensure account security.

### b. Content Information

We collect information you share or interact with on the Platform:

* Photos, videos, and text
* Product listings and brand details
* Reels, stories, Nordezon promotional content (ads)
* Comments and reviews
* Chat messages

### c. Device & Technical Data

Collected to provide app functionality and improve performance:

* Device tokens for push notifications
* Device identifiers, app version, and OS type (via Firebase Analytics)

> ❌ We do not collect IP addresses, browser data, or other unnecessary device identifiers.

Your information helps us to:

* Operate, maintain, and improve the Platform
* Facilitate product browsing, orders, and delivery coordination
* Personalize recommendations and shopping experiences
* Enable social features (likes, comments, messaging)
* Manage Brand profiles and interactions
* Provide analytics and insights
* Improve security and prevent misuse

> Online payments may be added in the future, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

* **With Brands:** To process orders and engagement
